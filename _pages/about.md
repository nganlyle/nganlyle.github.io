Welcome to my corner of the web!

I am a Canadian infectious diseases physician. I practiced as an adult infectious diseases clinician in British Columbia, Canada for a number of years before going back to school to complete a Masters in Data Science at the University of British Columbia Okanagan. 

Going back to school after many years, and then majoring in a field that I had little prior experience with, was an adventure! However, the experience has opened my eyes to a different way of thinking. 

Currently I work as a research fellow within the [Dengue Group](https://www.oucru.org/dengue/) at the Oxford University Clinical Research Unit ([OUCRU](https://www.oucru.org/)) in Vietnam. I am investigating how waveform data that is continuously collected in real-time, from wearable devices can be used to help in the management of dengue disease. For me, this is a dream opportunity, as the clinical and data aspects of this work are highly integrated.
